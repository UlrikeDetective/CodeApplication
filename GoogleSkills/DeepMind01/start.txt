Research Skills Focus
Reflection
Reflection is a critical research skill that involves the active process of thinking about and analyzing your own research process, decisions, and experiences. It goes beyond simply carrying out the steps of a research project; it involves a deeper engagement with the work, with the goal of gaining insights, improving understanding, and enhancing future research practices. By reflecting on what worked, what didn't, and why, researchers can identify areas for growth, refine their methodologies, and develop a more nuanced and sophisticated approach to inquiry.
